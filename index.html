Step-by-Step

How to Activate Your Card Online

Five simple steps between you and a fully activated card. Hover over each step to explore.

1

Visit the Official Activation Page

Open your browser and go to americanexpress.com/in/activate. Always verify the URL before entering any personal information.

2

Register for Online Services

New Amex customers will be prompted to create an account. Existing users can sign in directly.

  • Enter your card details
  • Verify your identity
  • Create a User ID and secure password
3

Verify Your Identity

Amex confirms your identity using your date of birth, card details, and a One-Time Password sent to your registered mobile number.

4

Create or Set Your PIN

Set a unique 4-digit PIN during activation. Avoid obvious combinations like your birth year or repeated digits.

5

Confirm Card Activation

A confirmation message will appear once all steps are complete. Your card is now active and ready for purchases, online transactions, and rewards.

Troubleshooting

Common Activation Problems

Click any issue below to see the likely cause and the fastest fix.

Card Details Not Recognized

⌄

Possible reasons: Incorrect card number, typing errors, or the card hasn't propagated in Amex systems yet.

Fix: Double-check all entered details carefully and try again after a few minutes.

OTP Not Received

⌄

Possible reasons: Weak network signal, incorrect mobile number on file, or delayed SMS delivery.

Fix: Request another OTP or contact Amex customer support to verify your registered number.

Website Not Loading

⌄

Possible reasons: Browser cache, temporary maintenance, or an unstable connection.

Fix: Refresh the page, clear your browser cache, or try a different browser on a stable connection.

Identity Verification Failed

⌄

Possible reasons: Name mismatch, incorrect date of birth, or wrong mobile number entered.

Fix: Ensure all details match your Amex records exactly — full legal name, correct DOB, and your registered mobile number.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is card activation free?
+
Yes. American Express does not charge any fee for activating your card through any of its channels.
How long does activation take?
+
Most cards are fully activated within a few minutes of successful identity verification.
Can I activate without creating an online account?
+
Yes. You can activate through the Amex Mobile App or by calling the customer service number on the back of your card — no online account required.
Can I activate a replacement card online?
+
Yes. Replacement and renewed cards can be activated through the Online Services portal or the Amex app using your existing credentials.
What if I forget my Online Services password?
+
Use the "Forgot User ID or Password" link on the American Express login page to securely reset your credentials.
